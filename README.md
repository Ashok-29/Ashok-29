### About Me

I am an **Assistant Professor of Computer Science & Technology** transitioning from academic AI research and teaching into applied **Machine Learning & Computer Vision engineering**. With two years of hands-on PyTorch deep learning R&D at **AMI Lab, Pusan National University** (ultrasound, dual-energy CT, and photoacoustic imaging) and peer-reviewed publications in ***IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging*** and ***MICCAI***, I build clean, modular, reproducible AI systems.
