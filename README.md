# Hi, I'm Ashok Kambaluru

**Assistant Professor (Computer Science & Technology) | Aspiring Machine Learning & Computer Vision Engineer**

I hold an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from Pusan National University and have two years of hands-on PyTorch deep learning R&D experience across ultrasound, CT, and photoacoustic imaging. I currently teach Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Introduction to Machine Learning while mentoring undergraduate AI-healthcare teams and building open, reproducible computer vision and ML systems.
